# **How I Created My AWS Free Tier Account**

## **Step 1: I Went to the AWS Website**
I visited the [AWS Free Tier page](https://aws.amazon.com/free/) and clicked **"Create a Free Account."**

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the AWS Free Tier homepage showing the "Create a Free Account" button._

## **Step 2: I Entered My Account Information**
- I provided my email address.
- I chose an AWS account name.
- I clicked **"Verify Email Address"** and entered the verification code sent to my email.

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the AWS sign-up form where email and account name are entered._

## **Step 3: I Created My Password**
- I set a strong password and confirmed it.
- I clicked **"Continue."**

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the password setup page._

## **Step 4: I Provided My Contact Information**
- I selected **Personal** or **Business** as my account type.
- I entered my full name, phone number, country, and address.
- I agreed to AWS’s terms and clicked **"Agree and Continue."**

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the contact information form._

## **Step 5: I Entered My Payment Information**
- I provided my credit/debit card details for identity verification.
- AWS charged a small temporary authorization fee (e.g., $1) to verify my card.
- I clicked **"Verify and Continue."**

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the payment details form._

## **Step 6: I Verified My Identity (Phone Verification)**
- I entered my phone number and country code.
- I chose **Text Message** or **Voice Call** for verification.
- I entered the verification code received and clicked **"Continue."**

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the phone verification page._

## **Step 7: I Chose a Support Plan**
- I selected a support plan (**Basic Free Tier** was recommended).
- I clicked **"Continue."**

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the AWS support plan selection page._

## **Step 8: I Signed In to AWS Console**
Once my registration was complete, I signed in to the [AWS Management Console](https://aws.amazon.com/console/) to start using AWS Free Tier services.

_Image Suggestion: Screenshot of the AWS Console homepage._
